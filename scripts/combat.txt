# Combat check: a wave of enemies in the range (the paper targets are replaced). Enemy indexes for
# aim=tN / cam=eN, in spawn order: 0-2 Mafioso (Plain), 3-4 SemiCleaner (Normal), 5-6 BlackOps (Sharp).
# The wave starts facing away from Max, idle. Mode changes are printed ("enemy N: mode").
# Crouching in cover is in scripts/combat_cover.txt (it needs Max low and lined up with the crate).
# Times are the sim clock; it slows with bullet time. Max is in god mode until (h).
# Enemies shoot each other when lined up, so who takes cover and who dies can differ if the AI changes:
# re-read the "enemy N: mode" lines and adjust the cam=eN lines.
#
# (a) idle poses, one enemy of each style up close
0.0 combat god=on weapon=0 cam=e0 slowmo=off
0.3 shot=a1_idle_mafioso
0.4 cam=e3
0.7 shot=a2_idle_semicleaner
0.8 cam=e5
1.1 shot=a3_idle_blackops
# (b) Max fires two shots at enemy 0: the noise alerts them all, they turn and aim at him
1.2 cam=player
1.5 mash aim=t0
1.62 cam=e6
1.7 shot=b1_alerted_turning
1.8 cam=e1
2.0 aim=t0
2.05 shot=b2_aiming_at_max
# (d) enemy 0 runs for cover (about 2 s)
2.1 cam=e0
2.5 shot=d1_run_to_cover
3.2 shot=d2_run_to_cover
3.7 shot=d3_run_to_cover
# (c) bullet time, seen from Max's side: enemy tracers in flight
4.4 cam=side
4.5 bt aim=t1
5.1 shot=c1_tracers
5.5 shot=c2_tracers
5.6 bt
# (e) one deterministic hit, god off: red edge flash and the direction mark
5.7 cam=default god=off
5.8 hurt=35
5.85 shot=e1_hit_flash
6.2 shot=e2_hit_mark
# (f) painkiller: the HUD count drops and health climbs
6.6 painkiller
6.7 shot=f1_painkiller
7.8 shot=f2_healing
# (g) shootdodge with the MP5 at enemy 3, god back on; the MP5 fire sends enemy 6 to cover
8.6 god=on weapon=5 aim=t3
9.0 fwd shootdodge fire aim=t3
9.5 fire aim=t3
10.0 shot=g1_dodge_fire
10.6 fire aim=t3
11.2 aim=t3
11.5 shot=g2_after
# (h) god off, one huge hit: death message
11.8 god=off
11.9 hurt=500
12.6 shot=h1_death
13.2 quit
